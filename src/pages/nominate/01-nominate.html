Nominate a Business

Know a local business that deserves the spotlight?

Nominations are free and open to any registered local business, in every industry.

What happens next

  1. We confirm the nominee

    Our team verifies the business is registered and local to the city.

  2. They join the ballot

    Confirmed nominees appear on the official ballot in their category.

  3. The community votes

    Winners are decided by verified community voting. We'll let you know when voting opens.
