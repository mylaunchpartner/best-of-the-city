Every Season

Three stages, from nomination to celebration

Nominating is always free, and results come from the community, not from what a business spends with us.

  1. Nominate

    Anyone can nominate a registered local business in their city: restaurants, salons, service providers, creative professionals and more. It takes about two minutes. Our team confirms each nominee is registered and local before it reaches the ballot.

  2. Vote

    Confirmed nominees appear on the official ballot by category. Winners are decided by verified community voting, and votes are reviewed for fairness and authenticity before results are final.

  3. Celebrate

    Winners receive official recognition on our platform, a physical award and marketing materials to display. They can also choose a professional media package to share their story; it never affects results.
