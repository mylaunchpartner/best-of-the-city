How It Works

Nominated by neighbors. Chosen by the community.

Every season runs city by city, in three simple stages.

  1. 01

    Nominate

    Anyone can nominate a registered local business in their city — restaurants, salons, service providers, creative professionals and more. Nominees are confirmed before they reach the ballot.

  2. 02

    Vote

    Confirmed nominees appear on the official ballot by category. Winners are decided by verified community voting, backed by quality checks for fairness and authenticity.

  3. 03

    Celebrate

    Winners receive official recognition on our platform, a physical award and marketing materials to display — plus the option of a professional media package to share their story.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do the Best of the City Awards work?

Each season, local businesses are nominated across multiple categories in their city. Once confirmed, nominees appear on the official ballot for community voting. Winners are recognized on our platform and receive a physical award and marketing materials by mail.

How are winners selected?

Winners are determined by verified community voting combined with quality checks that keep results fair and authentic. Purchasing a media package has no effect on nominations, votes or results.

What do participating businesses receive?

Every participant receives official recognition as part of the Best of the City Awards, a digital certificate and an online feature. Winners also receive a physical award by mail, and can choose to add a premium media package with a recorded award presentation, video storytelling and photography.

Can any business be nominated?

Yes. Any registered local business can be nominated, across every industry — from restaurants and salons to service providers and creative professionals.

Does it cost anything to be nominated?

No. Nominations and voting are always free. Media packages and growth services are optional.
